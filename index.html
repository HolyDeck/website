HolyDeck
GitHub

A full setup for Sunday.

Bible verses and song slides — prepared during the week, presented without worry.

HolyDeck is in early development. The first release is being built in the open.

What it will do

Prepare in minutes

Write the verse list for the service once. HolyDeck renders clean, ready-to-paste slide text for every translation your church reads.

Sunday never breaks

A local bible datastore keeps every verse on your own machine, so the service doesn't depend on the internet being up.

Present live (planned)

Verses and song slides on the second screen, driven from one place — the whole service in one flow.

$ holydeck get "PSA 118:24" --translations KJV

It starts at the command line; a full interface for the worship team follows.
